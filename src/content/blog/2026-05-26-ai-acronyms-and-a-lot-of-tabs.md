It has been a bit of a whirlwind lately.

I have been properly diving into the AI world now, reading blogs, working through articles, watching talks, and going through training material from different AI providers. Some of it is brilliant, some of it is useful, and some of it feels like someone just stapled buzzwords together and hit publish.

I am genuinely enjoying the shift towards AI-first, or maybe more accurately, agentic-first development. There is something really fun about moving from writing every single brick by hand to directing the build and checking what comes back. It feels less like typing and more like steering.

I can already feel the workflow changing, and honestly, in a good way.

The hard part is the speed of it all. Every day there is a new model, a new framework, a new “must-read” thread, and twelve fresh takes on what the future is supposed to be by Friday. Keeping up is one challenge, but filtering what is actually relevant versus what is just hype is another level.

Still, I am finding my rhythm slowly. Read, test, ignore the noise, repeat.

Anyway, still learning, still tinkering, still mildly confused, and still having a good laugh with it all.

Bye for now.
