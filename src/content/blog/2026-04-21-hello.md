I would imagine Every (older) developer eventually ends up writing one of these, jaded or not .... 

I've been meaning to start a proper(ish) blog for a while. Not a polished publication — just somewhere to put down thoughts, document things I'm figuring out, and occasionally rant about software in a semi-coherent way, AI musings - my "journey into ai" and so on.

This blog itself is a product of my nacent attempts to transition into ai as seemlessly as possible. To be honest its an adventure, an exciting one, also a steange experience in my years to offload the heaviy lifting to an anonymous agent. "Agentic engineering" is the new buzzword, I will have to adapt to the reality that my trusted old moniker of "Software developer" is now royally replaced with "Agentic" developer or similar. 

Quite frankly the move is riddled with new terms and anacronmys, a new slang to learn, not much dissimilar to the heady days of writing my first Java, but this time, the control is implicit, its not me having to write the code, its me having to understand how to "direct" the circus, the father becomes the son as so they say..

So here it is. Plain and simple. Even this small offering of an Astro built blog is a result of me in deep discourse with Github copilot, one of my faves so far, but theres more to come. Lucky to work for a tech savvy company that opens up many of the AI offerings for us, and is pushing us into the ai ecosphere. So its a work in progress - the AI helping me keep the paltform spick and span (avoiding defaulting to some bloated cms) and I make my mark by actually writing the blog posts by hand, well yes but im sure AI has a better grasp of grammar than my quick musings. 

So bare with me, its going to be a living creature as I find out more, and leanr more as well as realise my potential shortcomings of years of habits now needing to be relearnt in the new world. Ill add more features as they come. as my companionship with folk like Claude get a bit more amicable perhaps!

Thanks for stopping by, and keep in touch!
